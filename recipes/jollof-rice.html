Jollof Rice

description

Jollof rice is a popular nigerian dish made with rice,tomatoes,pepper,onions,and spices.It is delicious and commonly served at parties and family gatherings

ingredients

  • Rice
  • Tomatoes
  • Red pepper
  • Onions
  • Vegetable oil
  • seasoning cube
  • Chicken or meat

Steps

  1. Blend the tomatoes, pepper, and onions.
  2. Cook the tomato mixture to make the sauce.
  3. Add seasoning and spices.
  4. Add rice and cook until it is soft.
  5. Serve with chicken or your preferred protein.
