Egusi Soup

Description

Egusi soup is a traditional Nigerian soup made with ground melon seeds,vegetable, meat, fish, and spices. it is commonly eaten with pounded yam,eba,or fufu. Egusi

Ingredients

  • Ground egusi (melon seed)
  • Palm oil
  • Spinach or ugu leave
  • Meat
  • Dry fish
  • Stockfish
  • Seasoning cubes
  • Pepper
  • Onions
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Cook the meat and fish with seasoning.
  2. Heat the palm oil in a pot.
  3. add the cooked meat, fish, and stock.
  4. Add the vegetable and simmer for a few minutes.
  5. Serve with your preferred swallow.
